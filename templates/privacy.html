Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Spendly exists to help you understand your own spending, so the information you record here is personal by nature. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have over it.

01

Data We Collect

We collect the account details you provide when you register — such as your name and email address — along with the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates and any descriptions you add.

We also collect a small amount of technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device type and the pages you visit within the Service. We do not collect bank credentials, card numbers or payment details, and we never connect to your bank accounts.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the Service: to authenticate you, to store and display your expenses, and to generate the summaries, category breakdowns and date-range views that make your spending easier to understand.

We may use anonymised, aggregated information to diagnose problems and improve the Service. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense records to target advertising.

03

Data Storage

Your records are stored in the Service's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

You can delete individual records at any time, and closing your account removes the data associated with it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

04

Third Party Services

We rely on a small number of third party providers to run the Service, such as hosting providers and web font services. These providers may process limited technical data on our behalf and are expected to handle it in line with their own privacy practices.

We do not share your expense records with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may disclose information where we are legally required to do so, or where it is necessary to protect the rights and safety of our users.

05

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or you would like to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, get in touch at privacy@spendly.example and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
